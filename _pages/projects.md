# Projects

My research projects combine atmospheric observations, numerical modeling, data assimilation, and computational methods to investigate air quality and atmospheric composition.

A major focus of my work is the **Megalopolis of Central Mexico**, where I study the interactions between meteorology, emissions, atmospheric chemistry, and pollutant transport. My projects also involve satellite observations, ensemble and variational data assimilation, statistical methods, and machine learning.

---

## Current Projects

### Peroxyacetyl Nitrate (PAN) Transport and Ozone Stability in the Megalopolis

**Role:** Principal Investigator  
**Funding:** PAPIIT – UNAM  
**Status:** Ongoing  
**Start:** 2026

This project investigates the contribution of transported peroxyacetyl nitrate (PAN) from urban areas of the Megalopolis to the stability of ozone levels in Mexico City.

---

### Air Quality Forecasting and Chemical Data Assimilation

**Role:** Lead researcher for the chemical and meteorological data assimilation component  
**Funding:** Comisión Ambiental de la Megalópolis (CAMe)  
**Status:** Ongoing  
**Period:** 2024–2026

This project focuses on the development of an ensemble-based system for early warning of air pollution episodes and air quality forecasting.

My contribution focuses on the assimilation of atmospheric composition and meteorological observations for the Megalopolis of Central Mexico, including the use of satellite observations and numerical modeling.

## Recent Projects

### Remote Sensing Data Assimilation for the Megalopolis

**Role:** Principal Investigator  
**Funding:** PAPIIT – UNAM  
**Project:** IA105423  
**Period:** 2023–2024

Development and application of data assimilation methods using satellite observations of atmospheric composition for the Megalopolis of Central Mexico.

The project focused on integrating remote-sensing observations with atmospheric models to improve the representation of the atmospheric state.

---

### Ensemble Data Assimilation for the Megalopolis

**Role:** Principal Investigator  
**Funding:** LANCAD-UNAM-DGTIC  
**Project:** LANCAD-UNAM-DGTIC-395  
**Period:** 2022

Application of ensemble-based data assimilation methods to atmospheric modeling and air quality analysis in the Megalopolis of Central Mexico.

---

### COVID-19 Lockdown and Air Quality

**Role:** Principal Investigator  
**Funding:** UNAM Supercomputing Program  
**Period:** 2020–2021

Numerical modeling of the changes in ozone precursors, fine particulate matter, and secondary organic aerosols during the COVID-19 lockdown.

The project investigated the sensitivity of air quality to changes in anthropogenic emissions and provided modeling support for the interpretation of observed air-quality changes during the lockdown period.

---

### Air Quality Modeling Capacity Building in Mexico

**Role:** Co-Principal Investigator  
**Funding:** UCMEXUS–CONACYT  
**Period:** 2019–2020

Development of training activities and computational capacity for air quality modeling in Mexico.

The project included workshops and training activities focused on numerical atmospheric modeling, including WRF and WRF-Chem.
