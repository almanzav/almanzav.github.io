# Research Projects

My research projects combine atmospheric observations, numerical modeling, data assimilation, and computational methods to investigate air quality and atmospheric composition in Mexico.

A major focus of my work is the **Megalopolis of Central Mexico**, where I study the interactions between meteorology, emissions, atmospheric chemistry, and pollutant transport.

# Current Projects

## PAN transport and ozone stability in the Megalopolis

**Role:** Principal Investigator  
**Funding:** PAPIIT – UNAM  
**Period:** 2026–present

**Project:** *Evaluar la contribución del Peroxiacetil Nitrato (PAN) transportado de las zonas urbanas de la Megalópolis sobre la estabilidad de los niveles de ozono en la Ciudad de México.*

This project investigates the role of transported peroxyacetyl nitrate (PAN) in the regional chemistry of ozone and its contribution to the persistence and stability of ozone concentrations in Mexico City.

The work combines atmospheric chemistry, numerical modeling, and analysis of meteorological and chemical conditions across the Megalopolis of Central Mexico.

---

## Chemical Data Assimilation for Air Quality Forecasting

**Role:** Principal Investigator / Component Lead  
**Funding:** Comisión Ambiental de la Megalópolis (CAMe)  
**Period:** 2024–present

**Project:** *Ensamble para alerta temprana de episodios y pronóstico de calidad del aire.*

This project develops data assimilation capabilities for incorporating meteorological and atmospheric composition observations into air quality forecasting systems.

The work focuses on the assimilation of chemical observations using the WRFDA-Chem framework and related ensemble approaches, with applications to early warning and air quality forecasting over the Megalopolis of Central Mexico.

## COVID-19 Lockdown and Air Quality

**Role:** Principal Investigator  
**Funding:** UNAM–DGTIC Supercomputing Program  
**Period:** 2020–2021

**Projects:**

- *Impacto del periodo de confinamiento por COVID-19 en precursores de ozono, partículas finas y aerosoles orgánicos secundarios.*
- *Impacto del periodo de confinamiento en la calidad del aire: Modelación de escenarios de sensibilidad de emisiones de precursores de ozono y partículas finas.*

These projects investigated the changes in atmospheric composition associated with the COVID-19 lockdown and the substantial reduction in human activity and emissions.

Numerical modeling was used to evaluate the sensitivity of ozone, PM₂.₅, and secondary organic aerosol to changes in precursor emissions across the Mexico Megalopolis.

# Earlier Research Projects

## Air Quality and Industrial Emissions in the Mexico Megacity

Research conducted during my doctoral and postdoctoral work focused on the influence of industrial emissions on regional and local air quality in the Mexico City Metropolitan Area.

Particular emphasis was placed on emissions from the **Tula Refinery**, including elevated flaring and its contribution to SO₂, ozone, and particulate pollution in the Mexico Megacity.

This work involved the use of chemical transport models, meteorological modeling, emissions inventories, and observations from the MILAGRO field campaign.

---

## Air Quality Modeling and Model Evaluation

Throughout my research career I have worked with numerical atmospheric models including **WRF, WRF-Chem, WRFDA-Chem, WRF-FLEXPART, and related modeling systems**.

These activities have included:

- evaluation of meteorological model performance;
- evaluation of chemical transport models;
- comparison with surface monitoring networks;
- comparison with satellite observations and reanalysis products;
- sensitivity analysis of emissions;
- regional pollutant transport;
- operational air quality forecasting.

---

## Computational and Statistical Methods

My research also includes the development and application of computational methods for atmospheric science, including:

- variational data assimilation;
- ensemble Kalman filtering;
- statistical bias correction;
- machine-learning methods;
- high-performance computing;
- scientific programming;
- reproducible computational workflows.

These methods are increasingly integrated into atmospheric modeling and forecasting systems.
